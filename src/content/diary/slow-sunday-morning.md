Some mornings ask for nothing. The light comes in sideways, the kettle clicks off, and the whole day feels like a blank page that doesn't need to be filled yet.

I made tea, forgot about it, and made it again. I read three pages of a book and then just looked out of the window for a while. It felt like the most productive thing I'd done all week.

## little things i noticed

- the way the curtains glow when the sun hits them
- a bird that kept coming back to the same branch
- how quiet everything is before the world wakes up

I think I want more mornings like this. Not every day has to be a big day. Some days can just be soft.
