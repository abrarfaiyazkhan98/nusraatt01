Someone once told me to keep a list of tiny happy things, so on hard days there's proof that good days exist. So here's mine, started today.

1. Laughing so hard I couldn't finish a sentence.
2. The first sip of something warm on a cold evening.
3. Hearing a song I love in a place I didn't expect.
4. A message from a friend that just said "this reminded me of you."
5. Fresh flowers on a table.

None of these are big. All of them matter.

I'll keep adding to this list. Maybe one day it'll be long enough to fill a whole book.
